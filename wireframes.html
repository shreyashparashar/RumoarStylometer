Stylo Meter · Wireframes

Low-fidelity map of every screen in the Stylo Meter, the add-on to rumoar.com that answers the ASK moment: "does this look good on me?". Orange tags mark the key rule on each screen. The working prototype is at the app.

Drop your fit→Scan: face blur + on-device vision→Occasion→Vibe→Pick a stylist→Verdict: score, voice, moves, 3 RUMOAR picks→Share card / Shop on rumoar.com

Scoring pipeline

1 · Quality checkResolution, exposure, orientation.
2 · Person & poseFull body in frame. (MVP: MediaPipe Pose)
3 · Face blurOn-device, before scoring. Built.
4 · Garments & accessoriesMVP: Marqo-FashionSigLIP. Demo: user-declared.
5 · Five-pillar rubricMVP: Gemini Flash-class vision. Demo: palette heuristics.
6 · Jury calibrationAdjusts toward women's votes. Weight grows with data.
7 · Catalogue matchRanks RUMOAR pieces by real score gain. Built.
8 · Try-on renderMVP: Nano Banana 2, on tap only, low-res preview first.
